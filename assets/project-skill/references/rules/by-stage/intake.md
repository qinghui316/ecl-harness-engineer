## HR-13: Keep Coordination Local

Do not introduce daemons, remote locks, cross-machine synchronization, automatic Git initialization, or automatic business merges.

**On violation:** Remove the unsupported mechanism and report the local-only boundary.

## HR-23: Bound Clarification Rounds

Ask at most three high-impact questions in one round; record low-risk unknowns as assumptions and block only on unresolved high-impact unknowns.

**On violation:** Stop the interview, preserve answered decisions, and ask only the remaining material questions in a later round.
